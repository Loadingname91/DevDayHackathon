Quilt, a new app for iPhone, Android and the web.
Describe the app you need, like course notes with a folder per class, a journal, a workout log or a car maintenance tracker, and Quilt's assistant builds it in seconds. Your apps can share data, so a workout lands on your calendar and a purchase lands in your budget.
Fill any app from anywhere: type, talk, snap a whiteboard or a handwritten page, drop in a file, or connect another service. The assistant puts it in the right app and folder; you check the draft and tap OK.
Everything syncs live between your phone and your laptop.
Free: 5 apps and about 50 assistant actions a month. Plus: $6 a month ($48 a year) for unlimited apps and about 1,000 actions. Pro: $12 a month adds connectors to other services.
