Quilt, a new phone app. Android and web now; iPhone later.
Take a photo of a receipt, a syllabus or your fridge, and Quilt's assistant updates your budget, grocery list and due dates. You check the changes and tap OK; nothing saves without you. No bank login.
Starter apps for money, groceries, classes, to-dos, workouts and subscriptions, ready in one tap.
Free: 5 apps and about 10 photo fills a month. Plus: $6 a month ($48 a year) for unlimited apps and about 200 photo fills.
