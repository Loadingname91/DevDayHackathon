## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $81 a month (Vercel Pro, 2 members $40, Supabase Pro $25, Apple Developer Program ($99 a year) $8, Domain and email $8).

- **Break-even: never (each unit loses money) paying student-months a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (500 a day): **-289%** of every sale, after every cost.
