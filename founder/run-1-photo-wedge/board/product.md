# Product lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong

- **It starts from a moment.** Photograph a receipt, tap OK once, and Groceries, Budget and Calendar all update. That is an experience you can design backwards from.
- **The one sentence exists:** "Notion makes you build it. Quilt fills itself from a photo."
- **Draft-then-confirm is good craft.** The AI never writes behind the user's back. That matters to a buyer who won't hand over a bank login.
- **Apps keep working when credits run out.** Someone designed for the edge, not just the happy path.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)

1. **Capture that fails.** Fail twice and they go back to Notes. Everything runs through one handoff: messy photo, then AI, then a correct draft. A wrong total, a missed item or a slow spinner breaks the only thing that beats Notes. First-try accuracy and time-to-confirm are unmeasured.
2. **No focus.** Seven app types, four tiers, credit packs, automations, voice, connectors, bring-your-own-key, Teams and a marketplace. That is a platform roadmap on top of a product whose first moment is unproven, and every extra item thins the polish on the receipt beat.
3. **Credits meter the magic.** The fill is the value, and the fill is what gets metered. 50 free credits buy about 10 photo fills a month. Whether that is enough to form a habit is unknown.

## The questions I would ask the founder

- If you could ship one beat only, which is it?
- What do the first five minutes feel like with no buying history yet?
- On 50 real student receipts, how many are right first try, and how long is photo-to-OK? (Your own test, then the consumer panel.)
- Phone-first, but Android and web only: how many target students carry an iPhone? (Consumer panel.)
- What does one photo fill cost, and does a free user lose money? (CFO.)
- Which apps does a student still open in week four? (Measure with first users.)

## If FUND IF: the conditions

1. Launch with the receipt beat only: Budget and Groceries. Park the marketplace, Teams, connectors, bring-your-own-key and automations until that beat keeps users.
2. Set a capture bar and hit it before growth: first-try accuracy, and photo-to-confirm faster than typing it into Notes.
3. Keep credits out of the free user's way: no counter on the main screen, and a failed capture costs nothing.
4. Ship iPhone, or show from the consumer panel that Android plus web reaches the target students.
