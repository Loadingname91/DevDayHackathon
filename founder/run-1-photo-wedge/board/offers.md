# Offers lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The buyer is easy to reach.** U of U student groups, a survey and a waitlist are a cheap, direct channel to a tightly defined buyer.
- **The pain is concrete.** The "forgetting tax" (renewals, late fees, spoiled groceries, missed deadlines) is specific, and so is what makes these buyers quit.
- **Time delay and effort score well.** One receipt photo and one tap updates Budget, Groceries and Calendar. Seconds, nothing to build, no bank login. "Notion makes you build it" picks the effort fight, which is the right one.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The buyers have little money and a free fallback.** Students managing money for the first time are price-sensitive, and the Notes app costs nothing. Willingness to pay is unknown (consumer panel).
2. **Likelihood is unproven.** Two failed captures send users back to Notes. With no proof that photo fills land right the first time, perceived likelihood stays low, and that is where most offers die.
3. **The offer sells credits, not an outcome.** Four tiers, per-action credit costs, packs, Teams and a marketplace ask a student to do arithmetic before getting a result. Plus at $6 sits beside Todoist Pro at $5 and Notion's free tier, so the comparison becomes price. Whether 50 free credits (about 10 photo fills) reach a first win, and whether $6 covers 1,000 credits of AI cost, is unknown. The CFO answers both.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which forgetting-tax loss costs a student the most in a semester? (consumer panel)
- What does one active student cost in AI calls a month? (CFO)
- What result does a new user see in the first five minutes, and does the free tier reliably get them there?
- What would you guarantee? For example: if Quilt doesn't catch one renewal or deadline this semester, next semester is free.
- Could Plus charge double and still be the obvious choice? If not, what is missing from the stack?
- Do competitors discount for students? (competitor scout)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Willingness to pay is measured** before prices are fixed. (consumer panel)
2. **One offer named after the outcome**, such as a semester plan bundling syllabus import, renewal alerts and the rebuy list, priced per semester, with credits kept out of the pitch. Teams and the marketplace wait.
3. **A guarantee tied to a result**, and real urgency from the start of term.
4. **AI cost per active user sits below the price**, with headroom on the free tier. (CFO)
