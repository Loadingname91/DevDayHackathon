Quilt Home

Revised after an independent PM review from Sam's point of view. Home answers three questions in order: does anything need me, what's next, and how much is left. Then it lists every app. Nothing repeats, nothing is counted before Sam says OK, and Clair is one tap away: type, talk or add a photo.

What Home is made of

Built from signals every app emits, so it works for any mix of apps. A section with nothing to say disappears.

    What changed after the review

    • Cut "Right now". It repeated the status line and Next up. Clair's time-of-day judgement now decides what the status line says.
    • Nothing counts before OK. The receipt card shows "$412 → $376" and no app is marked changed until Sam adds it.
    • The Clair button replaces the old camera and ask buttons: one place to type, talk or add a photo, and one thing can land in several apps. Tap opens the Clair sheet; hold to talk. Finding an app is the find box.
    • Clair asks first in the evening: "3 things from today" waits in the queue like everything else.
    • Plain buttons: "Add to 3 apps", "Review items", "Remind me to cancel". Stitching lives in the visuals.
    • One card style for everything waiting (basted = waiting for OK), one at a time with "N more".
    • Your apps has a find box and a number on every patch; forgotten apps say "not opened in 3 wk".
    • Money is in the status line in every state. The header clock is gone.

    Theme

    Chrome stays muslin and ink so the colour comes from Sam's own apps. Waiting for OK is always the loose basting stitch, Clair is denim, "just changed" is mustard, and nothing is red.
