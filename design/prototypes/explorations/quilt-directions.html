Receipt Quilt

Every tab is a strip of coloured receipt paper, and Quilt sews the strips together with a zigzag stitch. The demo's receipt doesn't just fill a tab: it becomes a new strip in your quilt. It turns down the category's default of soft cards on a cream ground.

WORLD
Denim backing, thermal paper in five pastel roll colours (one per tab), thermal-black ink, mustard thread. Azeret Mono prints the receipts; Hanken Grotesk carries Clair's sentences.
FIRST VIEWPORT
The new receipt strip feeds in at the top, the seam sews itself, and the budget strip below already reads $376 with $412 struck out.
SAM'S PATH
Snap (top right, always) → read rows on a white strip → confirm → the strip is sewn in and the tabs it touched light up.
SIGNATURE MOVE
Feed and sew: new data prints out like a receipt and gets stitched on. Clair's ideas are pinned on, not sewn, until you say yes.
REACH
A gym log reads as a workout receipt and a calendar as a daily tally. Sam already shares receipt-style screenshots (Receiptify), and a tab could export the same way.
HONEST RISK
Mono-heavy and money-flavoured. Gym and class tabs have to work hard not to feel like bookkeeping, and long strips need disciplined spacing to stay scannable.

Raised by the challengers it beat

  • Over budget is cross-hatched, never red (from the darkroom exposure record).
  • A changed strip keeps its "just changed" tag until Sam has seen it (from the gate board).
  • The dock shows closed tabs as paper edges; only the open tab prints in full (from the record sleeve).
  • Clair signs checked work with a round stamp (from the design annual).

Quilter's Workbench

Your current Patchwork canvas, upgraded so that how a quilter works decides how the app behaves. Chalk marks are ideas, basting is a draft waiting for a yes, and running stitch means saved.

WORLD
Unbleached muslin ground, your six pastel patches, denim for actions, mustard thread. Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Hanken Grotesk for body (as the pitch doc asks), DM Mono for numbers. Caveat retires; Clair speaks in chalk.
FIRST VIEWPORT
Greeting, then the basted receipt sits first, with long loose stitches until Sam taps "Stitch all 8".
SAM'S PATH
Basted rows → one tap → running stitch. The selvedge dots on the card show which tabs it fed.
SIGNATURE MOVE
Stitch tightening: on confirm, long basting dashes animate into short, even running stitches.
REACH
Every tab gets a quilt label ("pieced by clair · nov 14"), and each of the 9 blocks gets a traditional quilt-block mark.
HONEST RISK
This is the familiar one. A warm light ground with soft pastel cards is where most apps in this category land, so the stitch states have to carry the identity.

Departures

Home is a live board of everything coming up across all your tabs, ranked by time. When a receipt lands, its row moves into place and stays lit until you've seen it.

WORLD
Warm charcoal board, cream type, one amber for "changed". Tab identity is a small colour code per row. Chivo and Chivo Mono.
WHY IT COMPETES
Strongest on product clarity: "calendar of everything" and data moving between tabs are the whole home screen.
WHY IT DOESN'T LEAD
It drops the quilt and the warmth the brand promises, and a transit board reads cold to Sam.

Verdict: competitive (wins product clarity, loses audience fit). The lead keeps its "stays lit until seen" rule.

Quilt Map

Home is a map of your life. Each tab is a flat-colour patch that grows with how much you use it, and seams between patches carry the data. Controls are bright pills.

WORLD
Flat, unshaded colour shapes on pale water, one bold grotesk (Gabarito) at one weight, yellow, sky, lilac and apricot pills. Everything moves with a soft overshoot.
WHY IT COMPETES
Most playful and Gen Z-friendly. One glance shows which parts of life are busy.
WHY IT DOESN'T LEAD
A map is a second metaphor on top of a quilt, numbers lose legibility, and nine block types don't fit inside blobs.

Verdict: competitive (wins audience fit, loses product clarity).
