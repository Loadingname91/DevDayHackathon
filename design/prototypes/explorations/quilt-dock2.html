Quilt Dock Studies II

Quick access versus seeing everything. Here's what other products do, then three evolutions of the patchwork border you liked, each tested on the same four tasks at two times of day.

How others split "quick" from "everything"

Glance without openingiOS widgets and Smart Stack show the useful widget for the moment, rotating by time and habit.Quilt: live numbers on Home
Jump to a favouriteArc keeps Favorites as fixed square tiles; super apps keep 6–8 services up front with the rest under "More".Quilt: pinned patches in fixed places
Go back to what I just usedAlipay lists "Recently used" mini programs in order of use; WeChat's pull-down opens recently used mini programs.Quilt: a "just used" fold
Predicted for right nowiOS Siri Suggestions and Widget Suggestions surface apps by time of day, location and routine.Quilt: Clair's chalk slot
Browse everythingiOS App Library sorts every app into automatic categories; Grab leads with an "All services" grid.Quilt: the unfolded quilt, by pile
Find by nameApp Library, WeChat and Alipay all put search at the top of the "everything" view.Quilt: search in the border

The finding that changes our design: every product above keeps favourites in fixed places for muscle memory and puts "recent" and "suggested" somewhere separate. My first patchwork border used your 4 most recent apps, so it would reshuffle every day. The versions below pin the border and give recents and Clair's suggestion their own homes.
