Quilt Home

Home is built from signals every app emits, not from app widgets, so it works for any mix of apps Sam builds. It answers three questions in order: does anything need me, what's next, how am I doing. Then it shows the whole quilt. Switch states to see how it adapts.

What Home is made of

Top to bottom is the job in order: act, then next, then glance, then browse. A section with nothing to say disappears.

    Voice

    The status line under the greeting is the voice of Home: one or two short clauses, written from live data, the most useful fact first. Specific numbers, relative times, no judgement, no exclamation marks, no "it looks like".

    SAYPull day in 15 minutes. $376 left this month.
    NOTHey Sam! 👋 You've got a busy evening ahead!
    SAYEating out is $15 over. Groceries is on track.
    NOT⚠️ You overspent on eating out!
    SAYAll caught up. Next: pull day at 6 pm.
    NOTNo new notifications.
    SAYWeeks you scan receipts, you spend $41 less eating out.
    NOTAI insight: it looks like there may be a correlation…
    • Section names are plain: Waiting for you, Next up, Right now, Clair noticed, Your quilt.
    • Clair speaks in first person only inside chalk ("I read 8 items"); Home never calls Clair "she".
    • Money over budget: say the amount and the category, never "you overspent".
    • Wins are stated once, plainly ("Hulu cancelled · $120 a year kept"), never as streaks.

    Theme

    Home's chrome stays calm muslin and ink so the colour comes from the apps themselves: the quilt at the bottom is the most colourful thing on the screen, and it is different for every student. Clair is always denim chalk, "just changed" is always mustard, and nothing is red.
