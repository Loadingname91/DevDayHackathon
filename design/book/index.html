CHAPTER 1

One app for the apps you'd otherwise forget

Quilt builds the apps you describe and joins them up: snap a grocery receipt and your groceries, budget and calendar all update. Clair, the assistant, never writes code; it fills a checked spec from nine blocks, so apps don't break.

First userSam, a University of Utah student who pays a "forgetting tax": renewals not cancelled, late fees, food that goes off, deadlines that sneak up. Opens apps in 5 to 30 second bursts, one-handed.
Job for Home"When I open Quilt with a spare few seconds, I want to see what needs me, what's next and how I'm doing, without opening anything."
PrinciplesCapture beats building. Connection is the product. Clair proposes, Sam approves. Ready apps before a blank canvas. Honest about money and privacy. No red.
CHAPTER 2

Quilter's Workbench

How a quilter works decides how the app behaves. A dotted chalk outline is an idea, a long basting stitch is waiting for your OK, a short running stitch is saved. Colour comes from the apps themselves; the frame stays muslin and ink.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque 800 for display and the one big number per app. Hanken Grotesk for all UI. DM Mono with aligned digits for every other number.

Motion

120 ms feedback, 220 ms state, 350 ms layout, 600 ms for the one focal moment (the thread pull). Exits faster than entrances. No bounce.

Loading

Clair's status line is the only loading UI. App switches never show a loader: cached values appear at once, first visits show basting where content will go.
CHAPTER 3

Clair, the spool buddy

Clair's face is a thread spool with eight expressions, each tied to a stitch state so the face and the stitches always agree. One spool per screen, never on app switches, money warnings or dense lists. UI copy never uses a pronoun for Clair.

CHAPTER 4

Home: act, then next, then everything

Home is built from signals every app emits, never from per-app widgets, so it works for any mix of apps. Revised after an independent PM review from Sam's point of view: no repeated information, nothing counted before OK, and the camera one tap away.

What Home contains

  • Status line: money left and the next thing, always.
  • Needs your OK: receipts and Clair's suggestions in one basted card style, one at a time.
  • Next up: a stitched thread through the next three dated things from every app.
  • Your apps: find box, most used first, a number on every patch.
  • Pinned bar: four fixed apps plus Snap/Ask (tap for camera, hold for Clair and search).

Voice

CHAPTER 6

Clair builds an app

Five screens, then you land in the new app. Clair builds first and only asks when the request is truly ambiguous; you pick one of three layouts and fine-tune; connections to your other apps get their own visible step.

CHAPTER 7

Search, rows and settings

One search everywhere, scoped to where you start, with filter chips generated from the app's own fields. Rows fix in seconds. Settings make the pitch's trust promises visible.

CHAPTER 8

Laptop: the same apps, wider

Split view with the same components. When the phone adds the receipt, the laptop shows "just changed" tags; the thread pull plays only on the phone that acted.

CHAPTER 9

What we decided

AreaDecision
WorldQuilter's Workbench: chalk = idea, basting = waiting for OK, running stitch = saved. Light only for DevDay; no red anywhere.
WordsClair builds apps; sections inside an app are tabs. Buttons say what happens ("Add to 3 apps", "Remind me to cancel"); stitching lives in the visuals.
TypeBricolage Grotesque, Hanken Grotesk, DM Mono. Caveat and DM Sans retired.
Identity8 dyes × 4 finishes = 32 patches; icon + name always; neighbours never share a dye.
ClairThe spool buddy is Clair's face; 8 expressions; one per screen; Proud only for real savings or records.
HomeStatus line → Needs your OK → Next up → Your apps → pinned bar with Snap/Ask. "Right now" cut after PM review.
BuildDescribe → Build → Preview (3 layouts) → Arrange → Connect and add → Ready. Example rows in the preview, cleared on add.
SearchPull down or hold Snap/Ask; scoped to where you start; chips from the app's fields; Ask Clair answers.
RowsTap for edit sheet; swipe right complete, left delete; long-press multi-select; undo everywhere.
TrustActivity of every Clair change with undo; what Clair can read (full, field names, nothing); export; credits meter.
LaptopSplit view: apps | app | detail; building = conversation | preview.
PersonaSam, the forgetting-tax student; student gig worker held as the "who pays most" answer.
Still open
  • 11 older canvas screens (All apps, Search, Welcome, Empty, Clair chat, Build, Clair building, Rule, Workout, Log a set, To-dos) are still in the old Patchwork style; their Workbench versions live in the flows prototype.
  • Swipe and drag were tested with a mouse in headless Chromium, not on a real touch screen.
  • "Stitch" comprehension is untested; the plain button labels are the safe default until 5 students try it.
  • DESIGN.md is written last, from the built Next.js app.
