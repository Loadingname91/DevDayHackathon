GLOSSARY.md

Quilt

Quilt is a phone-first workspace where Clair, an AI, builds small personal apps from a fixed set of blocks and stitches them together so one capture updates every app it touches. This glossary fixes the words we use for that, in the product and in the docs. The reference architecture (TabOS v3) uses some different words; they are listed under Avoid.

Language

Apps and their parts

App: One thing Sam keeps track of (Budget, Groceries, Workout), made from blocks and shown as one patch on Home. Avoid: Tab (TabOS v3 and the pitch doc), widget, mini-app

Block: One piece of an app, from a fixed set: page, text, metric, progress, collection, list, table, calendar, form or chart. Avoid: Component, module, primitive (in product copy)

Collection: The kind of record an app holds, with named fields (Groceries has items and trips). Avoid: Table, schema, database

Record: One entry in a collection: one grocery item, one set, one due date. The app shows it as a row. Avoid: Item (except in Groceries copy), entry, object

Field: One named value on every record of a collection (name, amount, due date, list). Avoid: Property (in product copy), column, attribute

List: A named group of records inside one collection, picked with chips (Weekly shop, Taco night). It is a value of the record's list field, not a separate collection. Avoid: Using "list" for the list block's layout (call that the list view)

View: How a block shows a collection: as a list, a table or a calendar. Avoid: Layout, mode

Face: The one headline value an app shows on its patch and in the status line ("$376 left", "4 to buy"). Avoid: Summary, badge, KPI

Usual: A grocery item Sam buys again and again, marked on the item itself (yes, no or worked out by Clair). Avoid: Staple, favourite, regular

Dates and repeats

Due date: The one main date a record sits on in Calendar and Next up. A thing with several dates becomes several records. Avoid: Deadline (in copy), timestamp

Repeat rule: How often a record comes back (every Saturday, every month on the 22nd), stored on the record. Avoid: Recurrence, schedule, cron

Occurrence: One date produced by a repeat rule. Completing it moves the record to the next occurrence. Avoid: Instance, copy

Clair and changes

Clair: Quilt's AI. It reads what Sam gives it (words, voice, photos, files) and Sam's apps, then answers, asks or proposes changes. Avoid: Assistant, bot, the AI, agent (in product copy)

Proposal: A set of changes Clair wants to make, shown as a card and saved only when Sam says OK. Avoid: Suggestion, draft, recommendation

Basted: The state of a proposal that is shown but not saved; nothing in any app has changed yet. Avoid: Pending, draft, unconfirmed

Stitched: The state of a change set that is saved into every app it touches. Avoid: Applied, committed, confirmed

Change set: Everything one gesture, one confirm or one undo changes, saved or undone together. Avoid: Command group (TabOS v3), transaction, batch

Stitch: A link that carries records from one app into another (a grocery trip becomes Budget spending). Avoid: Connection, integration, sync, relation

Clair note: Something Clair keeps about how Sam likes things done ("stopped buying Greek yogurt"), listed in Settings and deletable. Facts about Sam's things live in the apps, not in notes. Avoid: Memory, preference, profile

Menu question: A question Clair asks with tap-to-pick options before it proposes anything. Avoid: Prompt, survey, clarification

Check-in: Clair's short evening card listing what to log or decide today. Avoid: Notification, digest, reminder

Home and the bar

Home: The first screen: status line, Needs your OK, Next up, Clair noticed and Your apps. Avoid: Dashboard, feed

Patch: An app's tile on Home, in its dye, showing its face. Avoid: Card, tile, icon

Bar: The strip at the bottom of every screen with four pinned apps and the Clair button. Avoid: Dock, tab bar, nav bar

Pin: An app's place on the bar. Avoid: Shortcut, favourite

Accounts

Workspace: Everything one person owns in Quilt: their apps, records, notes and history. Avoid: Account (for the data), space, quilt (in technical docs)

Guest account: The account made the moment someone first opens Quilt, before they add an email or Google. It lives on that device until they keep their quilt. Avoid: Anonymous user, trial

Join code: A six-digit code that opens the same workspace on a second device. Avoid: Pairing code, PIN

Flagged ambiguities

  • The pitch doc says "tab" where the product now says app. TabOS v3 also says "Tab". Use app everywhere a person reads it.
  • "Row" is how a record looks in a list; use "record" in docs and "row" only when describing the UI.
  • "List" means a group of records (Weekly shop). The list-shaped layout is the list view.
  • "Memory" was used for both Clair notes and data in apps. Facts about Sam's things are records (or the usual mark); how Sam likes Clair to behave is a Clair note.
