Clair, the spool buddy

One character, eight expressions, each tied to a stitch state so the face and the stitches always agree. Every face at 96, 44 and 30 px. UI copy never uses a pronoun for Clair.

See them move

Where Clair shows up

Yes: while Clair reads or builds, when something needs your OK, in empty states and onboarding. One spool per screen.

Never: app switches, money warnings, dense lists. A sad face next to an overspend reads as judging.

Sizes: 30 beside the status line, 44 on cards, 96 in empty states. Nothing in between.

Small sizes (44 and under) get heavier outlines, bigger eyes, no barrel bands, and cues outside the barrel. Small “from Clair” marks on cards use the chalk instead.

Clair

The Clair button (the bar’s right cell) is a faceless spool mark, not the character: no face, never animates, never counts as the screen’s spool.

Motion and loading

Hop420 ms, up 5 px and back, once. The whole spool moves from its base, same distance at every size. Shake360 ms, ±3 px with a 3° tilt, once. Bob2 px, every 560 ms, only while working. BlinkA double blink about every 4.3 s, only while working. OnceHop and shake play only when the face changes, never on a re-render. Talk The listening line is a running stitch; stitch lengths follow your voice. ReducedNo movement. The face alone carries the state; talk shows a still line and “Listening”.

Clair’s status line is the only loading UI. App switches never load: cached values show at once; first visits show basting where content will go.
